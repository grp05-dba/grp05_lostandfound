## Limits

No blocked activity was visible in Part A. No slow-query incident was established from the extract; disabled duration logging prevents using absence of such messages as proof of good performance. Part D measures one safe SELECT directly.

Coverage is limited to the readable service unit/window and sessions with a correlatable authorization record. Pre-authorization failures, sessions opened before the window, rotated history, other sinks and unlogged behavior may be absent. Database fatal-session counters are not a comprehensive authentication audit. No application HTTP logs were reviewed.

Raw SQL, DETAIL/HINT, credential values and unrelated-project messages were excluded from retained evidence. There were no changed log access rights or server settings. The existing runtime restriction remains an explicit limitation rather than an incident to suppress.
